---
title: Top-ups with iPaymu
description: Fund your organization balance through Indonesian payment rails.
---

Top-ups are created and paid from the **Top ups** section of
[app.sintaks.dev](https://app.sintaks.dev). The customer portal handles
authentication, payment-channel selection, buyer details, idempotency, payment
instructions, and status tracking.

QRIS, virtual accounts, convenience stores, cards, paylater, and e-wallets
are available through the same portal flow. After iPaymu verifies the payment,
the organization balance is credited exactly once.

The portal manages the complete payment flow; customers do not need to call any
account-management endpoint directly.
