---
title: Top up your balance
description: Fund your organization balance step by step; the invoice lands in your inbox.
---

Top-ups are created and paid from the **Top up** section of [app.sintaks.dev](https://app.sintaks.dev). The portal handles authentication, payment-channel selection, buyer details, payment instructions, and status tracking for you — you do not need to call any account-management API.

## Step-by-step top-up

### 1. Sign in to the portal

Open [app.sintaks.dev](https://app.sintaks.dev) and sign in with your account.

### 2. Go to the Top up page

From the sidebar or dashboard, open **Top up**.

### 3. Enter the amount

Type an IDR amount. Each order has a minimum of **Rp10.000** and a maximum of **Rp10.000.000**. You enter the amount in whole Rupiah.

### 4. Choose a payment channel

Select the payment method you want to use: **QRIS**, **virtual account**, **e-wallet**, **convenience store**, **card**, or **paylater** — whichever is available on your channel list. The portal shows the instructions for the selected channel.

### 5. Complete the payment

Follow the on-screen instructions (for example, scan the QRIS code or transfer to the shown virtual account). The portal tracks the payment status and confirms it automatically.

### 6. Balance is credited

Once the payment clears, your organization balance is credited **exactly once** — retries, duplicates, and out-of-order callbacks are handled so you are never credited twice.

### 7. Check your inbox for the invoice

A receipt/invoice email is sent to your account email for every successful top-up. Keep it for your records, reconciliation, and support reference.

## Need help?

If a payment does not appear within a few minutes, open a support request from the portal's **Need Help** widget on the Top up page — include the order details so we can trace it. Every charge that is confirmed on our side is visible in your transaction history.
